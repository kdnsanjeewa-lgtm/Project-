Types of Computers

Explore the different computers we use every day, discover what makes each one special, and learn where they are useful.

📘 What is a computer?

A computer is an electronic device that accepts data, processes it according to instructions, stores information, and produces useful results. Computers come in different shapes and sizes because people use them for different tasks.

💻 Five types of computers

🖥️

1. Desktop computer

A desktop computer is designed to stay on a desk. It usually has a separate monitor, keyboard, mouse and system unit. It is useful in offices, schools and homes.

💻

2. Laptop computer

A laptop is a portable computer with a screen, keyboard, touchpad and battery built in. You can carry it and use it in different places.

📱

3. Tablet computer

A tablet is a lightweight computer with a touchscreen. People use it to read, watch videos, draw, study and browse the internet.

📲

4. Smartphone

A smartphone is a mobile phone with computer features. It can run apps, take photos, connect to the internet and help people communicate.

🚀

5. Supercomputer

A supercomputer is an extremely powerful computer that can process huge amounts of data. Scientists use supercomputers for weather forecasting, research and complex simulations.

🔎 Quick comparison

⭐ Remember!
Computers can be designed for different needs. A desktop is suited to a fixed workspace, a laptop or tablet is easy to carry, a smartphone fits in your pocket, and a supercomputer handles very complex tasks.

1. Which computer is designed to be easy to carry and has a built-in keyboard?
A laptop computer.
2. Which device commonly uses a touchscreen and is larger than a smartphone?
A tablet computer.
3. What type of computer is used for very complex scientific calculations?
A supercomputer.

A monitor and keyboard. A mouse and system unit are also common parts.

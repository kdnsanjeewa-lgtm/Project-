Lesson 03 • Connecting computers

🌐 What is a Computer Network?

Discover how computers connect, communicate and share resources — from a classroom to the whole world!

🎉 Welcome to Lesson 3!

Let's explore computer networks and learn how connected devices help people share information.

🎯 Learning goals

✓ Explain what a computer network is
✓ Identify LAN and WAN
✓ Describe network uses and benefits

📘 What is a computer network?

A computer network is a group of two or more computers connected together to share information and resources. Computers can connect using cables or wireless connections such as Wi-Fi. Networks help people communicate and exchange information quickly and easily.

🧩 Small parts that help a network work

A network is made up of connected devices and equipment. Here are a few important examples:

🧵

🔌 How can devices connect?

🧵 Wired connectionDevices connect using physical cables, such as Ethernet cables. Wired connections are often reliable and are commonly used in computer labs and offices.
📶 Wireless connectionDevices connect without a physical data cable, often using Wi-Fi. This makes it convenient to connect laptops, tablets and phones around a home or school.
🔐 Network passwordMany wireless networks use a password to help prevent people who are not allowed from connecting. Keep your home network password private.

🛡️ Use networks safely

🔒 Protect passwordsUse strong passwords and never share them with strangers.
👀 Think before sharingDo not share private information with people you do not know.
✅ Ask for helpIf something strange happens online, tell a parent, teacher or trusted adult.

🧭 Types of computer networks

🏫

LAN — Local Area Network

A LAN connects computers in a small area, such as one home, school building or office. It is useful for sharing files and devices nearby.

🌍

WAN — Wide Area Network

A WAN connects computers and smaller networks over large geographical areas, such as across cities, countries or continents.

🌐

The Internet

The Internet is a huge worldwide network of connected networks. It allows people around the world to communicate and access information.

⭐ Benefits of computer networks

📁 File sharing: People can share documents and pictures.
🖨️ Resource sharing: Many computers can use the same printer.
💬 Communication: Users can send messages and exchange information.
🤝 Teamwork: People can work together and share resources.
⏱️ Save time: Sharing information can be faster and easier.
💡 Remember:
LAN = Local / small area. WAN = Wide / large area. Internet = Worldwide network of networks.
⭐ Lesson 3 summary
✓ A computer network connects two or more computers.
✓ Networks help share information and resources.
✓ Connections can use cables or wireless signals.
✓ LAN is used in small areas, such as a school or office.
✓ WAN connects computers over large geographical areas.
✓ The Internet is a worldwide network.
🏆 Fun fact: The Internet connects billions of devices around the world. It is much bigger than a network in one school or office!

📝 Quick quiz

Think about each answer, then open the question to check!

1. What is a computer network?
A group of two or more computers connected to share information and resources.
2. What does LAN stand for?
Local Area Network.
3. Which type of network connects computers over large geographical areas?
WAN — Wide Area Network.
4. Give one example of a resource that can be shared on a network.
A printer, files or documents are examples.
5. What is one difference between wired and wireless connections?
Wired connections use physical cables. Wireless connections send data without a physical data cable, often using Wi-Fi.
6. What does a network switch do?
It connects several wired devices together in a local network.
